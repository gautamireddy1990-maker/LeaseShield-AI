COMMERCIAL RETAIL LEASE AGREEMENT

Sunrise Plaza · Suite 104 · Effective January 1, 2027

12. OPERATING EXPENSES AND COMMON AREAS

Tenant shall pay, as additional rent, Tenant's proportionate share of all Operating Expenses incurred by Landlord in connection with the ownership, operation, management, maintenance and repair of the Project.

Landlord may include an administrative fee equal to fifteen percent (15%) of all such costs. Operating Expenses may include capital improvements and replacements that Landlord deems necessary for the Project.

Tenant's share shall be adjusted annually based on actual costs. Landlord shall provide a statement of such expenses within a reasonable time following each calendar year.

13. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

Tenant shall maintain the HVAC equipment serving the Premises in good condition, including replacement of components as necessary.
